2. Description of Service

Hayato On Fire is an automated utility application designed for the Discord platform. Its primary functions include:

  • Dynamic role assignment and removal based on numeric Discord Role IDs.
  • Customizable direct message (DM) notifications rendered from server-configured templates.
  • Administrative audit logging to track role changes, executors, and DM delivery statuses.
  • Role hierarchy and permission verification to prevent unauthorized role escalation.
